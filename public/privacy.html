ReelTara Privacy Policy

Draft for developer review — 19 October 2026

ReelTara is developed by CÔNG TY TNHH CÔNG NGHỆ VÀ DỊCH VỤ NVB This policy explains the current free movie discovery and open-film playback features.

Data stored on your device

ReelTara stores your country setting, watchlist, viewed and trailer history, and playback progress locally on your device. There is no account registration or cloud synchronization in this release. You can erase this information by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it.

Internet services

Movie information, artwork, video playback and official links may connect to our application API and third parties such as TMDB, Internet Archive, Wikimedia Commons, Blender Foundation, YouTube or linked streaming providers. When you access their services, the providers may receive technical connection data including IP address and device information, subject to their privacy notices.

Advertising and billing

This version does not integrate an advertising SDK or in-app purchases. If introduced, we will update this notice and applicable consent choices.

Children and films

This is a general-audience discovery app, not specifically targeted to children. Individual movies may contain material unsuitable for some ages.

Contact

Contact the developer via nvbtech.com.vn for privacy inquiries. A verified support email must be supplied before publication.
